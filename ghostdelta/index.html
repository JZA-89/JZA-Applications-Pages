GHOSTDELTA

EVERY SESSION. EVERY LAP.

GhostDelta is a precision GPS timer for recreational speed — karting, mountain biking, skiing and snowboarding, motorcycling, boating, hiking, running. Record on Apple Watch. Analyse on iPhone.

Race yourself: live timing against your own records, sector by sector, with the delta on your wrist while you're still moving.

Stages

Timed sections, detected automatically

Define a Stage on any trail, road or track. Every Run is timed against your best with sector and mini-sector precision, and your Stage Book keeps every record.

Circuits

Every lap scored as you cross the line

Save any closed loop as a Circuit. Live lap timing with sector splits, session bests and all-time bests — and the delta while you're still driving it.

Navigation

The route on your wrist

Import a GPX route, drop named waypoints, and follow it from the Watch — distance remaining, the elevation profile ahead, the next climb with gain and gradient.

Activities

Karting, Motorsport, Motorcycling, Boating, Jet Ski, Snowmobile, Mountain Biking, E-Mountain Biking, Cycling, E-Cycling, Downhill Skiing, Snowboarding, Cross-Country Skiing, Running, Trail Running, Walking, Hiking, Climbing, Surfing, Sailing, Rowing, Paddle Sports, Open Water Swimming — or create your own, right on the Watch.

Your data

No account, no feed. Sessions live on your devices and export to GPX any time. Apple Health sync is opt-in, per session.
