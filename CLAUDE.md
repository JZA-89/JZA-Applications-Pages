# JZA Applications website — CLAUDE.md

The portfolio site for JZA Applications, Jaisah's indie iOS/watchOS studio. Live at **https://jza.app** (GitHub Pages, custom domain in `CNAME`, DNS at Cloudflare). Read `README.md` too.

## Who you're working with

- Jaisah is a structural engineer and a solo indie developer. He is **new to coding** but has strong product and design judgement. Explain what you changed and why in plain language, with no analogies. Keep it direct.
- He cares about detail and polish. Fit in with the existing look rather than "improving" the design system unless he asks.
- Each app has its own project in `~/Documents/<AppName>` (PowerGlass, PointFoundry, GhostDelta, BananaBomb, BikeSpec, Parlato, ThumbControl, MORPHOS). These usually include their own `CLAUDE.md` and `STYLE_GUIDE.md`. **They are the source of truth** for an app's features, colours and wording. Read them before writing copy or a demo, and never claim a feature you haven't confirmed in that app's code or docs.

## Hard rules

- **Hand-written static site.** Plain HTML/CSS/JS. No frameworks, no build step, no npm, no new libraries, no trackers or analytics.
- **Dark only.** `color-scheme: dark`.
- **Don't change the `<head>` meta tags** (`og:*`, `twitter:card`, canonical) on `index.html` without asking. They control link previews and were set up deliberately. `og-image.png` (1200×630) is the preview image.
- **Never set `overflow-x` on `html` or `body`.** It breaks the homepage scroll snapping. Clip overflow on the container instead (`.chapter` does this).
- **Every animation must:** respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (show a still frame instead), pause when offscreen (IntersectionObserver), and stay light enough for an older iPhone.
- **Privacy policies and App Store links matter legally and commercially.** Policy text must match what the app actually does: check the app's code. Don't remove or rename existing pages. App Store Connect links to their exact URLs (`<app>/support.html`, `<app>/<Name>PrivacyPolicy.html`); a renamed file breaks a live listing.
- **Cache-busting:** CSS and JS are linked with `?v=N` (e.g. `site.css?v=17`, `dock.js?v=12`, `main.js?v=18`). When you change one of these files, bump its number **in every HTML file that links it**: the homepage and all `<app>/*.html` pages. Check with `grep -rho "site.css?v=[0-9]*\|dock.js?v=[0-9]*\|main.js?v=[0-9]*" --include=*.html . | sort | uniq -c`; each file should show a single version.

## Homepage chapter pattern

Every app chapter in `index.html` follows the same shape. Copy an existing one rather than inventing a new layout:

- `<section class="chapter" id="<slug>" data-chapter="<slug>" data-app="<slug>">`. Alternate `chapter alt` on every other chapter, which swaps the sides on desktop.
- The copy column: kicker (`<b>NN</b> · devices`), icon (`.chapter-icon .vt-<slug>`), wordmark, description, About/Support/Privacy links, then the App Store badge.
- The art column holds a **live HTML/CSS replica of the app's real UI**, not a screenshot, drawn from that app's `STYLE_GUIDE.md`. Its JS gets its own numbered section in `main.js`.
- Elements use `.reveal` with a `--reveal-delay` for the staggered entrance.
- Each app also has an ambient light layer: `<div class="amb amb-<slug">` plus its CSS and `html[data-view="<slug>"]` rule near the top of `index.html`.

### Adding an app (checklist)

1. `assets/icon-<slug>.jpg`: 512px JPEG from the app's `AppIcon` 1024px PNG.
2. `site.css`: theme block `[data-app="<slug>"]`, wordmark `.wm-<slug>`, `.vt-<slug>`.
3. `index.html`: ambient layer + rule, chapter (next number), "All apps" strip entry, and the app name in `<meta name="description">`. Add the demo's root class to the `<noscript>` hide list if it's JS-drawn.
4. `dock.js`: an entry in `items`. Then, in `site.css`, update the number of dock items in the phone-width dock rule (`max-width: 480px`), so the icons still fit across a phone screen.
5. `main.js`: a new numbered section for the demo (reduced-motion still frame, IntersectionObserver pause).
6. `<slug>/index.html`, `support.html`, `<AppName>PrivacyPolicy.html`, modelled on an existing app's pages.
7. Bump the `?v=` numbers everywhere (see Hard rules).

In co-op the boss is sent whole, so any new boss field must be a plain value or be checked on its own in the receiver (`b.grab` is); a grab is removed with `delete`, never set to null.
  - **Hermit tasks (v34):** about half the sanctuaries have a hermit (`MorphosGame.hermitOf`, seeded; the first sanctuary always has one; sanctuaries keep at least three regions apart via `_rollsSanctuary`/`_sanctuaryYields`). Each hermit looks different (colour, beard, eyes, shell from its id, `drawHermit`). Entering a hermit's sanctuary with an offer or a reward waiting opens a dialog (`#hermitDialog`, `openHermit`, once per visit; the small card's Talk button reopens it) with a portrait, a story line (`TASK_STORIES`), the task's direction, distance, water level and reward, and Accept / Not now (or Claim reward with a thank-you, or Drop). Tasks (`_makeTask`) are 0.9–3.8k from the hermit in water no deeper than one level above the colony: hunt a named ancient (`elderHome` `'bounty:…'`), clear a nearby nest, claim a nearby place, rescue a lost relative who then follows you home (`fx`,`fy`,`found`), or recover a glowing relic (`carried`). Up to `MAX_TASKS` (3), one per hermit; done tasks are claimed back at the hermit (`claimTask`). One task is tracked (`state.trackedTask`, `trackTask`; tap a tracker row or Track in the Map tab): it gets a pulsing beacon on its target (following the marked ancient once it exists), an arrow at the screen edge when off screen (inside the play area), and a large marker or rim arrow on the minimap (`MorphosGame.taskPoint` gives the current target). Tracker rows show progress, distance and level. Map symbols must never look alike (v34.2): a task to do is a solid gold diamond; a task done (go back to the hermit) is a gold tick, on both maps, on the in-world beacon and above the hermit (the hermit's other marks are `!` for an offer and `…` while a task is running); the checkpoint is a pale flag; you and friends are arrows (white and cyan); places found are small dots; sanctuaries are green rings; wonders are gold stars; a gold `?` means only a rumoured wonder. Study leads and the old explore target are not drawn on the minimap. Offers stay honest (v34.3): a nest that has lost any of its family or a place already claimed is never offered, and a waiting offer whose target was done meanwhile is rebuilt (`_spentTarget`, `_offerFor`); hunt targets are `'bounty:'+hermit+':t'+n` and skip any id already in `eldersFallen` (older saves and a friend's kill could otherwise complete a hunt on accept); the place you just dropped is not offered straight back (`_lastDropped`); hunt, rescue and fetch spots keep 200 clear of any place; nest tasks are named by direction ("Clear the west nest"). A task from an older save whose hermit's sanctuary no longer exists moves to the nearest free hermit (`_nearestHermit`). Each hermit gives three tasks per life (`HERMIT_TASKS`, counted in `stats.helped` by sanctuary id when claimed); each reaches further out (0.9–2.2k, 1.5–3k, 2.2–3.8k from the hermit) and leans towards deeper water, and the reward tier follows effort (`MorphosGame.taskTier`: water, +1 for a trip over 2,500, +1 for a hunt; the old `final` bonus is gone). After that the hermit is `spent`: no `!` mark, and a line sending you to others further out. Claiming keeps the dialog open and shows the reward as a card (`showHermitReward`, from `game.lastReward`: icon, tier, name, body part, what it does) with Farewell and Equip (opens the Mutations tab); the usual found-mutation notice is skipped while the dialog is open. The hermit dialog never opens by itself: arriving at a sanctuary only shows the small card, and its Talk button opens the dialog. Saves check tasks (kinds hunt, nest, place, rescue, fetch) and convert an old single `bounty`; the hermit position is re-read from the sanctuary on the first tick (`hermitAt`). Rescue followers and relics are local to each player in co-op. In co-op a marked ancient killed by anyone completes every task naming it, and duplicate copies are removed (lowest id stays).
  - **In-game HUD (v30):** one cockpit in the bottom centre: health and stamina as liquid gauges (`.ck-orb`), the cell count with its growth ring (`.ck-gem`), nutrition as a thin bar, and the nine move buttons (the existing `.control-cluster`, restyled) between them. A navigation minimap (`drawMiniMap`, every frame; explored water on one reused layer, a line at each water-level boundary, places, boss and friends; no ordinary creatures; since v35.3 no rim notch towards the shallows, as the water-level lines show the way) sits top-left with the water level under it; the buttons sit top-right. Since v32.5 there is no navigation card in play (the minimap shows the target and opens the full map; `#quickNavigation` stays in the page, hidden). Since v33.1 the full map is the fourth tab of the pause screen (`buildMapTab`, `mapPanel`; the old `#navigationDialog` contents are moved into it at start-up and the dialog is never opened). `openMap()` opens it; Tab toggles it or switches to it; it zooms (wheel, pinch, + − buttons) and pans (drag) through `mapView`, and ◎ recentres, and the boss bar shows only the name and health. On screens wider than 16:9 everything stays inside a centred 16:9 frame (`--hx`), and the HUD scales with `--u`. Touch: a small cockpit between the thumbs in landscape, and a single top row (map, cockpit, buttons) in portrait. The old vitals bars remain only for screen readers.
  - **Saves** are version 20; versions 18 and 19 are converted on load (`fromSave`). Measurements such as reach are always recomputed, so a balance change does not invalidate a save.
- **Controller (v34.7):** outside play the d-pad moves focus spatially (`padPick`), except on the Mutations tab, where the six slots have fixed ring neighbours (`PAD_RING`: Mouth on top, Senses and Membrane on the left, Strike organ (`organ`) and Nucleus on the right, Flagellum at the bottom), Right from the right side enters the slot's mutations, Left from the first mutation returns to the slot, Down reaches Equip (`padMutationsMove`). Whatever the controller lands on there is also selected (`padFollow`). A on a slot moves the cursor into its mutations, starting on the one worn or the first (`padIntoSlot`); there Left/Right compare them, Up/Down stay inside the panel (between rows, then Equip), A equips or removes at once (`padEquipChip`), and B returns to the slot (`padOutOfSlot`) instead of closing the pause screen. On the Lineage tab the d-pad follows the drawn lines (`padTreeNeighbours`, `padTreeMove`): from a node it goes to the connected node (I to its fork pair and to the other classes' I through the centre, II to I, III, its sibling and the hybrids beside it, and so on) whose direction on screen is closest to the one pressed, and stays put when none is; landing on a node shows it (`padTreeFollow`). A on a node moves to its Evolve button when one is offered (choosing is permanent, so it takes a second A), B returns to the node, and after evolving the cursor returns to the node grown. Down from a tab enters that tab's content at its first control in reading order (v35.5), not whatever sits straight below the tab. In play, d-pad Up talks to a hermit while its card shows; the Talk button shows an ↑ key only while a controller is in use (`.pad-glyph`). Checked with scratchpad `test/padmut.js` and `test/padhermit.js` (a fake controller).
- **Stat points (v34):** the stat points counter in the pause screen header (`#skillBalance`) opens the Stats tab.
- **Text size rule (Jaisah's, v25.7):** no tiny text anywhere in the game's interface. In menus and dialogs nothing is smaller than 11px, and sentences are 12.5px or more (the Co-Op dialog uses 13px and up). In the in-game HUD, where a phone has little room, the floor is 10px. New interface text follows the same floors; the exception is the numerals inside the Lineage tree's small circles, which scale with the circle.
- Playing alone, the game's only network request is a version check: a few seconds after opening (and when the tab comes back into view, at most every 5 minutes) it fetches its own page, `morphos.html`, with the cache bypassed, reads the version from the `<title>`, and offers a reload if it is newer. Nothing is sent. Keep the title in the form `MORPHOS | Living Waters · vX.Y` or the check stops working.
- **Co-op (the Co-Op button, up to eight players)** connects the players' browsers directly to each other with WebRTC. Game data never passes through a server. The network requests it makes are:
  - `https://coop.jza.app`: a Cloudflare Worker on Jaisah's Cloudflare account (source in the MORPHOS project, `coop-worker/worker.js`, with a D1 database bound as `DB`). It only passes the two browsers' connection details to each other, deletes them when collected or after two minutes, and accepts requests only from `https://jza.app`. If the game is ever served from another address, the Worker's `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` must change too.
  - Public STUN servers (`stun.cloudflare.com`, `stun.l.google.com`), which tell a browser its own internet address.
  - The direct connections to the other players.
  - "Connect by copy and paste" in the same dialog skips the Worker.
  The privacy page describes exactly this. If co-op's connections change, or the game adds any other request (analytics, leaderboards), update `morphos/MorphosPrivacyPolicy.html` to match.
- **Down in co-op (v33.4):** the pond keeps running on a downed player's device (`_downStep`): the body stays where it fell, untouchable and unseen (stealth 1), its health, stamina, nutrition, growth and score are held, and its creatures keep living for everyone. Before this, `update` returned early while dead, freezing everything that device ran.
- **Drops in co-op (v33.7):** mutation drops are personal (each player's own copy, chosen for their own collection), but whether a creature drops is decided once, by the device that lands the kill (`_dropChance`, sent as `d` in the `k` message and applied as `o._dropForce`), so everyone who damaged it gets one or nobody does.
- **Friendly fire (v26.0)** is always on in co-op: a player's Jab, Bite, Command pulse, Surge, shots and Pounce hurt the other players they touch (`_ff`, message `ff`). The attacker's device decides the hit; the victim's device takes the damage through its own defences. Rams, poison and broodlings do not hurt friends. A friendly hit is capped at 34% of the victim's maximum health, does not give the victim general invulnerability (a separate 0.6 s `ffGuard`), and does not land while the victim is away (paused, evolving).
- A co-op pond is saved separately from a solo life (`localStorage` key `morphos.coop.v1`), so it also depends on the URL staying the same.

## Checking your work

- Run it locally with `python3 -m http.server` from this folder, then open `http://localhost:8000`.
- Check desktop (~1400px wide) and phone (~390px) widths. On the homepage, also check: scroll snapping lands on each chapter, the dock highlights the right app, every demo runs and pauses offscreen, and reduced motion shows still frames.
- Check the browser console for errors. The App Store badge images load from Apple's servers, so they fail offline; that's expected.
- Check the new app's About/Support/Privacy pages and the dock on another app's page.
