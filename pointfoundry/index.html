POINTFOUNDRY

ROOM & OBJECT 3D SCANNER

PointFoundry turns the depth sensors already in your iPhone and iPad into a proper 3D scanner. Use the LiDAR scanner to capture the spaces you walk around, or the TrueDepth camera to capture the objects you hold in your hand — as dense point clouds and reconstructed meshes you can view, clean up and export.

No cloud processing, no account, no waiting for an upload. Every scan is built and stored on your own device.

Room Scan

LiDAR · spaces you walk around

Walk through a room and PointFoundry captures it live as a point cloud, building up the geometry as you go. Ideal for interiors, layouts and larger objects you can move around.

Object Scan

TrueDepth · things you hold

Turn a single object on a table, or move around it, and PointFoundry tracks its pose to reconstruct it in the round — the detail you want for small, hand-held things.

Clean & view

Tidy the capture, see it your way

Trim stray points, smooth the cloud and switch between true-colour and height-mapped views in the built-in viewer, so a raw scan becomes something you can actually use.

Export

Your scan, your files

Export point clouds and meshes to standard formats including OBJ and STL, ready to drop into your 3D, CAD, printing or game pipeline. Share them anywhere you like.

Your data

PointFoundry has no account and no servers. Scans are captured and stored entirely on your device, and go somewhere else only when you choose to export or share them.

Pro

Unlimited scanning is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock. Purchases go through the App Store and are tied to your Apple Account, so they restore across your devices.

Available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad with a LiDAR scanner or TrueDepth camera.
