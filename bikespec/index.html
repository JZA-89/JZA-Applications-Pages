BikeSpec

Every bike, every spec, every setup.

BikeSpec keeps everything about your bikes in one place — the full specification, how each one is set up, what's been serviced, and the photos. It's the spec sheet and logbook you'll actually keep up to date, stored on your own device and synced through your private iCloud.

One bike or a whole stable — it's built to hold the detail that's otherwise scattered across manuals, receipts and memory.

Specifications

The whole bike, on record

Make, model, size, components, geometry, tyres and suspension settings — plus any custom spec items you want to track. As much or as little detail as you like.

Spec scanning

Photograph a spec sheet, keep the numbers

Point the camera at a manufacturer spec sheet or geometry chart and BikeSpec reads it with Apple's on-device Vision and Apple Intelligence, pulling the figures out for you. The photo and text never leave your device.

Setups & service

Every adjustment and repair, logged

Record setups and adjustments, keep a change log, and track service records so you always know what was done, and when.

Photos & bike images

Add your own, or generate one

Attach photos from your library, or create a bike image on device with Apple's Image Playground. Your prompts and reference photos stay on the device.

Range estimation

Import a route, size up the ride

Bring in a GPX route and BikeSpec works out the distance and climbing on device, so you can gauge whether a bike — or a battery — is up to it. Route points are used only for the calculation, never stored as a tracked ride.

iCloud sync

The same garage on every device

With iCloud enabled, your bikes sync through Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud account, staying consistent across your devices — with no account to create and nothing held by us.

Your data

No account, no servers, no analytics. Your bikes live on your device and in your own private iCloud — we can't see any of it, and nothing is shared with third parties.

Pricing

Free to use, with a one-time in-app purchase to unlock unlimited bikes. Purchases go through the App Store and are tied to your Apple Account, so they restore across your devices.
