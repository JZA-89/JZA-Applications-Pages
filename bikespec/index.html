BikeSpec

Every bike, every spec, every setup.

BikeSpec is a garage for mountain bikers that remembers every setting on every bike. It shows how each bike is set up right now, how far that has drifted from your reference, and what to change. It also keeps up with servicing. Everything stays on your iPhone and in your private iCloud, and it works with no signal.

The numbers you check at the trailhead (pressures, sag, clicks) are front and centre. BikeSpec never invents a value you didn't enter.

Setup

Nudge it, see it, save it

Every pressure, click and sag figure has its own + and − buttons, or tap the number to type it. Each one shows how far it sits from your reference, such as “3 psi under ref” or “on ref”. Changes wait until you save them, then go into the change log as one entry, and any of them can be undone later.

Named setups, with a checklist to switch

Keep a setup for each kind of riding, along with the rider weight it was set for. When you switch setups, BikeSpec lists exactly what to change on the bike, and you tick each item off as you go.

Tuning tools

Setup Genie

Tell it what the bike feels like, and it suggests fixes in order, based on fixed tuning rules for your fork, shock and the adjusters they actually have. Try one, ride, and say how it went. It moves to the next fix or takes the same fix a step further, and puts things back if a fix made it worse.

Sag Assistant

Measure the o-ring gap, or type the percentage. BikeSpec compares it with your target sag and suggests a new pressure or spring rate. Set it, measure again.

Trailhead and Temp Check

Trailhead shows your current numbers in large type, with a checklist, and keeps the screen awake. Temp Check tells you what to pump your tyres and air springs to at home, so they're right at the temperature you'll ride in.

Service

Know what's due

Fork and shock services, brake bleeds and sealant are each tracked on their own interval, and rings fill as each one comes due. Chains, pads and bearings wear by distance rather than time, so you get regular reminders to check them instead. A reminder appears on the due day, and the Service tab lists everything due across your bikes. Log who did each job and what it cost, and see your spend for the year.

Specs and geometry

The whole bike, on record

Frame, size, components, drivetrain, wheels, tyres, suspension and geometry, including flip-chip positions, plus any custom items you want to track. Add as much or as little detail as you like.

Scan a spec sheet or geometry chart

Photograph a manufacturer's spec sheet or geometry chart and BikeSpec fills in the fields, reading it on your iPhone with Apple's Vision and Apple Intelligence. Geometry charts are read size by size, anything that doesn't look right is left out rather than guessed, and a scan never overwrites what you've already entered. The photo never leaves your iPhone.

Compare two bikes

Lay two bikes, or two sizes, over each other as side-on outlines, static or sagged, with every geometry difference listed.

E-bikes

Range and motor

Plan a ride by typing in its distance and climbing, or by importing a GPX route. Log the battery each ride used, and BikeSpec learns how far a charge takes you. Motor Graph draws your motor's output from its own figures.

Photos, reports and sync

Your bike, shared the way you choose

Add photos from your library, or make a bike image on your iPhone with Apple's Image Playground. Export a PDF report with the spec, setup, geometry and service history, ready for a buyer, an insurer or a shop. With iCloud turned on, your garage syncs to your other devices through your private iCloud account.

Your data

No account, no servers, no analytics. Your bikes live on your device and in your own private iCloud — we can't see any of it, and nothing is shared with third parties.

Pricing

Free for up to two bikes, with a one-time in-app purchase to unlock unlimited bikes. Purchases go through the App Store and are tied to your Apple Account, so they restore across your devices.
