CAST IN STONE

A golem of living rock in a web of caves. Type elements to shape a spell: the first is its form, the rest change it. Explore out from the hub; the further you go, the tougher it gets. The cave and your golem are saved in this browser.

Two elements

Point at a spellThe first element you type is the form. Each one after it adds its property, in any order. Typing the form's own element again makes it bigger. Each element after the second costs more than the last. Up to seven.

Special spells

Controller: left stick run, right stick aim, B X Y A elements, RT shot, LT burst, LB jump and lift, RB move, d-pad up rests, d-pad down takes off the last element, View clears, Menu pauses.

Touch controls come later. For now, play with a keyboard and mouse, or a controller.
