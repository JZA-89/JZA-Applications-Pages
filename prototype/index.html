CAST IN STONE

A rock golem in a cave, with four elements. Load two: every pair gives you an aimed Shot, a Burst around you and a way to Move. Dig, build, burn, flood and shock your way around, try them on the training dummies, and fight the shale crawlers.

Every pair of elements gives you three spells: a Shot you aim, a Burst around you, and a Move. Jump, then keep holding Space to use the Move; steer it with W A S D, so you can move one way and shoot another. Loading a third element pushes out the first; your golem's eyes (or crystals) show what is loaded. Every spell costs mana, and held spells drain it. Mana refills on its own, faster on the ground.

Touch controls come later. For now, play with a keyboard and mouse, or a controller.
