CAST IN STONE

A rock golem in a web of caves, with four elements. Load any two: every pair gives you an aimed Shot, a Burst around you and a way to Move. Explore out from the hub; the further you go, the tougher it gets. Your world and your golem's progress are saved in this browser.

Type up to seven elements. The first is the form of the spell and the rest change it; longer recipes cost much more mana. What you typed stays loaded; after you cast it, the next element you type starts a new recipe. Some exact sequences are special spells, learned from tomes in the cave. Your own Lifts, Moves and Bursts never hurt you. Controller: left stick run, right stick aim, B X A Y elements, RT shot, LT burst, LB jump and lift, RB move, d-pad up rests, View clears, d-pad down takes off the last element, Menu pauses.

Touch controls come later. For now, play with a keyboard and mouse, or a controller.

Point at a spellThe first element you type is the form. Each one after it adds its property, in any order: fire burns, water soaks, earth adds weight, lightning chains. Typing the form's own element again makes it bigger. Each element after the second costs more than the last. Up to seven.
