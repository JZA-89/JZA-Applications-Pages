KILN

A rock golem in a cave, with four elements. Load two, then cast: every pair is a different spell. Dig, build, burn, flood and shock your way around, and try them on the training dummies.

Loading a third element pushes out the first. Your golem's eyes (or crystals) show what is loaded. Every spell costs mana; Steam and Water jet drain it while held. Mana refills on its own, faster on the ground. Run dry and you can't cast until it refills.

Touch controls come later. For now, play with a keyboard and mouse, or a controller.
