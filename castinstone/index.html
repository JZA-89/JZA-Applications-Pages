You are a golem of living rock, woken in a web of caves. Somewhere in it the Emberwyrm sleeps in a lake of magma, under three seals, and each seal is held by a champion. Break the seals, wake the wyrm and bring it down. Type elements to shape a spell: the first is its form, the rest change it. The further from the hub, the tougher it gets.

Your journey

Your golem

The first element you type is the form. Each one after it adds its property, in any order. Typing the form's own element again makes it bigger. Each element after the second costs more than the last. Up to seven.

Pick a spellEvery pair of elements gives a Shot, a Burst, a Lift and a Move.

Two golems in one cave. One of you hosts and gives the other a five-letter code. You play in the host's cave; each of you keeps your own golem, with its level, runestones and spells, and what you gain is saved to your own device.

or join:

In co-op the game does not stop while a menu is open: your golem stands still and cannot be hurt until you return.

A friend's spells hurt you a little. Enemies you kill together give experience to both of you when you are close.
