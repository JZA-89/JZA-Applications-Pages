ThumbControlBeta

Your button, your rules.

ThumbControl makes your iPhone's Action Button context-aware. Instead of doing one fixed thing, a press checks what's going on right now (the time, where you are, what's connected, what's on your calendar) and runs the action that fits.

Play music when your AirPods are in. Open the TV remote when you're home. Dim the house at bedtime. Open the shopping list at the supermarket. Same button, every time.

How it works

Rules, checked top to bottom

A tool is a list of rules. Each rule pairs one or more conditions with one or more actions. When you press the button, ThumbControl checks the rules in order and runs the first one that fits. If none do, it runs your fallback.

Tools for different parts of life

Keep separate tools, say Daily Life and Holiday, and switch between them with a tap. Only the active tool answers the button.

Build rules in plain English

Describe it, and it's built

Type something like "play music when I'm driving" and ThumbControl turns it into a rule, ready to check and add. It runs on Apple Intelligence, on your phone, so nothing you type is sent anywhere. Prefer full control? Build any rule by hand.

Conditions

  • Time of day
  • Location: near a place you choose, or your saved Home and Work
  • Calendar: during an event, outside one, or just before one
  • Activity: walking, running, driving or still
  • Audio: AirPods, CarPlay, Bluetooth, wired headphones or the speaker
  • How the phone is held: portrait, landscape, face up or face down
  • Battery level and charging
  • Network: Wi-Fi (or a particular network), cellular, or none
  • Whether music is playing

Combine conditions so they all have to be true, offer alternatives, or add exceptions ("but not when…").

Actions

  • Play an Apple Music playlist or station
  • Run a HomeKit scene
  • Run any Shortcut
  • Open an app, a Reminders list or a website
  • Jump straight to the camera, Wallet, Voice Memos, or Clock's timer and alarms
  • Navigate somewhere in Maps
  • Start a call or a message to a contact
  • Log water, weight or mindful minutes to Apple Health

A rule can run several actions in a row.

More than the Action Button

Run it from anywhere

Trigger your active tool from the Action Button, Back Tap, Siri ("Run ThumbControl"), the Shortcuts app or the Home Screen widget. A Test button shows exactly which rule would fire right now and why, and the History screen keeps a record of recent presses.

Your data

No account, no servers, no analytics. Your rules and history stay on your phone, and ThumbControl only checks your situation when you press the button. Read the privacy policy.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26. The Action Button is on iPhone 15 Pro and later; on other iPhones, use Back Tap, Siri or the widget. Building rules in plain English needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence.
