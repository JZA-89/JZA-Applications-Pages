ThumbControl

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ThumbControl ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. ThumbControl is developed by JZA Applications.

In short: ThumbControl works entirely on your phone. There are no accounts and no servers. Your rules, places and history stay on your device, and the app only checks your situation when you press the button. We do not use analytics or advertising, and we do not collect or share your information.

How ThumbControl works

When you press the Action Button (or trigger ThumbControl another way), the app checks the conditions in your active tool's rules, such as the time, where you are or what's connected, and runs the first rule that fits. All of this happens on your phone. Nothing about your situation, your rules or what the button did is sent to us or to any third-party server.

Permissions and what they're used for

ThumbControl asks only for the permissions your rules need, and you can turn each one off in Settings › Privacy & Security at any time. Rules that depend on a permission you've turned off simply won't match.

  • Location (only while using the app): to check whether you're near a place in one of your rules. Your location is read when you press the button and used for that check. Your coordinates are not stored or sent anywhere; the on-device press history notes only how far you were from the place in the rule.
  • Calendar: to check whether you're in, outside or about to start an event. Events are read on your phone and never sent anywhere. When a calendar condition is checked, the name of the event you're in may be noted in your on-device press history.
  • Motion & Fitness: to tell whether you're walking, running, driving or still.
  • Wi-Fi network name: when a rule depends on a particular Wi-Fi network, the name of the network you're on is read to compare against it.
  • Apple Music: to let you choose playlists and stations, and to play them.
  • HomeKit: to list your home scenes and run the one you choose.
  • Reminders: to list your Reminders lists so you can pick one to open.
  • Contacts: when you set up a call or message action, you choose one contact in Apple's contact picker. ThumbControl saves that contact's name and phone number in the rule. It does not read the rest of your contacts.
  • Health: to save water, weight or mindful minutes to Apple Health when a health action runs. ThumbControl writes this data only; it does not read your health data.
  • Notifications: to show a short confirmation when an action runs in the background. These notifications are created on your phone. You can turn them off in ThumbControl's Settings.

Building rules with AI

When you describe a rule in plain English, ThumbControl uses Apple Intelligence's on-device model to turn it into a rule. What you type is processed on your phone and is not sent to us or to any server.

Data stored on your phone

ThumbControl keeps the following on your phone only. It is not sent to us and is not synced to iCloud:

  • Your tools and rules, including any places, contacts, playlists, scenes, shortcuts, web addresses and messages you set in them.
  • Your saved Home and Work locations, if you set them.
  • A history of recent button presses: which rule ran, whether the action worked, and what each condition found, for example the time, how far you were from a place, the name of the calendar event you were in, the audio device connected, or the battery level. It is kept for up to 30 days or 200 presses, whichever comes first, and you can clear it at any time. It is only shared if you choose to copy it and send it to us yourself.
  • Your app settings.

The Home Screen widget reads your active tool from the same on-device storage.

Apple services

Some features rely on Apple services, which handle their own data under Apple's Privacy Policy: searching for a place on the map (Apple Maps), playing music (Apple Music), running home scenes (HomeKit), and Siri and Shortcuts. ThumbControl does not receive anything from these beyond what it needs to run your rule on your phone.

Actions that open other apps, such as Maps, Phone, Messages, Shortcuts or a website, hand over to that app, whose own privacy policy then applies.

Beta testing

If you test ThumbControl through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares limited information with us as the developer, such as the email address or name you joined with, your device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and we use it only to improve ThumbControl.

Data we collect

Outside of TestFlight, we do not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. Specifically, ThumbControl:

  • Does not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Does not run servers that receive your data.
  • Does not require an account, email address or password.
  • Does not track your location in the background.
  • Does not track you across apps or websites.

Children's privacy

ThumbControl is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your control over your data

  • Edit or delete any tool or rule in the app at any time, or reset everything in ThumbControl's Settings.
  • Clear your button-press history from the History screen.
  • Turn off any permission in Settings › Privacy & Security.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its data from your phone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, within the app or App Store listing.
