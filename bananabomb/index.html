BananaBomb 🍌💣

Swipe. Peel. Bomb.

BananaBomb turns cleaning your photo library into something you'll actually finish. One photo at a time: swipe right to peel (keep), swipe left to bomb (delete). That's it.

Built for the library you've been avoiding — the 40,000 screenshots, near-duplicates and blurry shots you'll never look at again.

Bunches

Duplicates, found automatically

BananaBomb fingerprints every photo with Apple's Vision framework and groups lookalikes into bunches. Review a bunch side by side, keep the best one, bomb the rest.

BananaBrain

On-device AI that learns your taste

Every swipe trains a preference model that lives on your phone. The more you peel and bomb, the better BananaBomb gets at serving up the photos you'll want gone — worst first.

Barry

Your banana-shaped hype man

Barry the Banana reacts to your swipes, celebrates your milestones, and — on devices with Apple Intelligence — comes up with his own commentary. Fully optional, entirely on-device.

Filters

Focus the queue on what you want to clear: screenshots, selfies, food, documents, people, animals, landscapes, videos, blurry shots or bunches — toggle any of them in or out.

You're always in control

Nothing is deleted behind your back. Bombed photos wait in a batch until you confirm the standard iOS deletion dialog, and deleted photos remain in Recently Deleted for around 30 days. BananaBomb keeps a running total of the storage you've reclaimed.

Your data

No account, no servers, no analytics. All photo analysis happens on your device — no photos ever leave your phone.

Available on the App Store for iPhone. Free to try; BananaBomb Pro is a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock.
