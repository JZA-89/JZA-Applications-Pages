Now we're talking.

Parlato is a hands-free intercom for people who are close together, first of all a motorcycle rider and their passenger. It uses just your phones and earbuds: no internet, no cell signal and no intercom hardware. Phones connect directly to each other over Wi-Fi, so you can talk to the person right next to you without shouting, wherever you are.

It suits anywhere people stay within a few metres of each other: the back of a motorbike, a tandem or a sidecar, a loud workshop, kitchen or job site, or a concert.

Talk like you're sitting together

Both talk at once

There's no push-to-talk and no waiting for a turn. Parlato picks up your voice when you speak and stays quiet when you don't. It re-learns the background noise within a couple of seconds as it changes, on top of Apple's echo cancellation and noise reduction.

Phone to phone

No network needed

Voice travels directly between phones over Apple's peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. There's no server in the middle, so it works the same with or without coverage.

Range

About 10 metres, the length of a bus. A little further in the open, less through walls and buildings. Parlato is made for people riding or sitting together, not for a group spread out down the road.

Reconnects by itself

If the link drops for a moment, or someone steps out of range, Parlato keeps looking for them and reconnects on its own when they're back, with a short sound so you know.

Two ways to talk

Chat

Talk with any of your contacts who are nearby. Friends of your contacts can join in too.

Private Chat

Just the people you pick. Each one is asked in, and nobody else can join. Up to eight people can share a chat, as long as everyone is within range of everyone else.

Your data

No account, no servers, no analytics. Your voice goes only to the people in your chat and is never recorded or stored. Read the privacy policy.

Pricing

Parlato includes 2 hours of free chat time. After that, a one-time purchase, Parlato Pro, unlocks unlimited chats, with no subscription. Friends can join your chats for free. While it's in beta, everything is free to test.
