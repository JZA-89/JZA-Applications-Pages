ParlatoBeta

Now we're talking.

Parlato lets people who are near each other keep talking hands-free, like an always-open walkie-talkie, with no internet, no cell signal and no accounts. Phones connect directly to each other over Wi-Fi, so the conversation carries on wherever you are: on the bike, on a trail, on a job site, or somewhere with no coverage at all.

Pop your phone in your pocket, put your earbuds or helmet headset in, and talk.

Talk like you're together

Everyone can speak at once

Parlato is full duplex: no push-to-talk and no waiting for a turn. It picks up your voice when you speak and stays quiet when you don't, with voice processing that cuts down background noise.

Phone to phone

No network needed

Voice travels directly between phones over Apple's peer-to-peer Wi-Fi. There's no server in the middle, so it works in the backcountry exactly as it does in town. Up to eight people can share a chat.

Built for pockets

Lock your phone and keep going

A chat keeps running with your phone locked for hours. If someone rides out of range, Parlato tells you, keeps looking for them, and reconnects on its own when they're back, with a short sound so you know.

Two ways to talk

Chat

Talk with any of your contacts who are nearby. Friends of your contacts can join in too.

Private Chat

Just the people you pick. Each one is asked in, and nobody else can join.

Your data

No account, no servers, no analytics. Your voice goes only to the people in your chat and is never recorded or stored. Read the privacy policy.

Pricing

Parlato will include free chat time to try it properly, then a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited chats. Friends can join your chats for free. While it's in beta, everything is free to test.
