Parlato

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Parlato ("the app", "we", "us") handles your information. Parlato is developed by JZA Applications.

In short: Parlato connects phones directly to each other. There are no accounts and no servers. Your voice goes only to the people in your chat and is never recorded or stored. We do not use analytics or advertising, and we do not collect or share your information.

How Parlato works

Parlato finds other phones running Parlato nearby and connects to them directly over Apple's peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, without a router, the internet or a mobile network. Nothing you say or do in a chat passes through us or any third-party server.

Your voice

  • When you're in a chat and speaking, your microphone audio is processed on your phone and sent directly to the other phones in that chat, where it is played and then discarded.
  • Parlato does not record, save or upload your voice, on your phone or anywhere else.
  • The microphone is used only during a chat. You can mute at any time, and you can turn off microphone access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

What nearby people can see

To let people find each other, your phone announces itself to other phones running Parlato within Wi-Fi range. That announcement includes:

  • The display name you choose (by default, your phone's name), and the badge you pick (an emoji or up to three characters), if any.
  • A random identifier created by Parlato on your phone, so the people you've added recognise you again. It is not linked to your Apple Account, phone number or any other identity.
  • Whether you're in a chat, and which kind (open or private), so your contacts can see when you're chatting.
  • Whether your copy of Parlato is unlocked, so your friends can join your chats for free.

Only people physically near you, with Parlato open, receive this. You can change your name and badge at any time in Parlato's Settings.

Data stored on your phone

Parlato keeps the following on your phone only. It is not sent to us and is not synced to iCloud:

  • Your display name, badge and app settings.
  • Your Parlato contacts: the names, badges and random identifiers of people you've added in the app. Parlato does not access your phone's Contacts app.
  • Your random Parlato identifier and the amount of free chat time you've used, kept in your phone's Keychain so they survive reinstalling the app.
  • A diagnostic log of connection events (for example, when someone joined or a link dropped), kept to help fix problems. It stays on your phone and is only shared if you choose to copy it and send it to us yourself.

Purchases

Parlato offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock unlimited chat time. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your name, payment card or other payment details. Your purchase is read from the App Store and tied to your Apple Account, which is how it restores across your devices. Making a purchase needs an internet connection; nothing else in Parlato does.

Purchases are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Calls and the Lock Screen

To keep a chat running while your phone is locked, Parlato uses Apple's CallKit, so a chat appears like a call in iOS, and shows a Live Activity on your Lock Screen. These are handled on your phone by iOS. Parlato does not access your phone calls, call history or phone number.

Beta testing

If you test Parlato through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares limited information with us as the developer, such as the email address or name you joined with, your device model and iOS version, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, and we use it only to improve Parlato.

Data we collect

Outside of TestFlight, we do not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal data. Specifically, Parlato:

  • Does not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Does not run servers that receive your voice or any other data.
  • Does not require an account, email address or password.
  • Does not access your location, photos or phone contacts.
  • Does not track you across apps or websites.

Children's privacy

Parlato is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your control over your data

  • Change your name and badge, or remove contacts, in the app at any time.
  • Turn off microphone or local network access in Settings › Privacy & Security.
  • Delete the app to remove its data from your phone. Your free-time count and random identifier stay in the Keychain so free time isn't reset by reinstalling.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, within the app or App Store listing.
