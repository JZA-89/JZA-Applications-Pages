Parlato

Support

Parlato is a hands-free intercom for a rider and passenger, or anyone within a few metres of each other, phone to phone, with no internet or cell signal. It's currently in public beta on TestFlight.

Common questions

Does Parlato need the internet or cell signal?

No. Phones connect directly to each other over Wi-Fi, without a router or mobile network. The only time Parlato uses the internet is for an in-app purchase.

Someone nearby doesn't show up

Check that Wi-Fi is turned on in Control Centre on both phones (they don't need to be joined to a network), and that Parlato is allowed to use the local network: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network › Parlato. Open Parlato on both phones and give it a few seconds to find each other.

How far apart can we be?

About 10 metres, the length of a bus. It reaches a little further in the open, and less through walls, buildings or people in between. Parlato is made for people riding or sitting together. If someone drops out of range, Parlato shows "Reconnecting" and joins them back automatically when they return.

Can I lock my phone during a chat?

Yes, that's how it's designed to be used. A chat keeps running with the phone locked, and shows on the Lock Screen while it's active.

How many people can be in a chat?

Up to eight. Everyone needs to be within about 10 metres of everyone else, because voice goes phone to phone and isn't passed along from one phone to the next.

Where's my data?

On your phone. Parlato has no accounts and no servers, and voice is never recorded. Details are in the privacy policy.
