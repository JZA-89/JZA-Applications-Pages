    Controller
    Left stick swims, right stick aims (and turns you on the spot). RT strikes, LT swims fast, LB blocks, RB sneaks (hold, or tap to stay hidden). Organs: A Siphon, X Venom, L3 Slipstream, Y Shell, B Decoy. Start pauses, View opens navigation. In menus: d-pad or left stick moves, A selects, B goes back, LB/RB switch tabs.
    Touchscreen
    Steer with the thumbstick that appears under your left thumb; strike, block, swim fast and sneak with the buttons under your right thumb. Strikes fly where the stick points, or the way you face when it rests; tapping open water strikes towards that spot. The DNA icon opens your build.

    Evolution. Each family banks its own experience from matching actions: graze for Grazer, hunt for Hunter, swim fast and escape for Swimmer, block and shield for Guardian, sneak near predators and ambush for Stalker. When a family's experience fills, it grows a rank on its own. Each new rank, in any family, raises the cost of the next, so focus on one family to go deep or spread out to go wide. At ranks II and IV a family forks: pick one of two specialisations on the Lineage tab whenever you like (play carries on), and the other closes for this life. After rank IV, experience grows mastery. Grow two neighbouring families to rank II and a hybrid forms between them. See it all on the Lineage tab.

    Divisions. Eating builds growth. When growth fills, your colony divides: twice the cells, a larger body, more health, and a stat point for the six core stats. Each division raises the stat rank limit. You can refund them at full health and stamina, outside elder encounters.

    Mutations. Six body parts each hold one mutation, opening from 4 to 64 cells. Earn mutations through play, from rare creatures, elders and ancient fossils, and swap them on the Body tab between encounters.

    Your Strike. Every cell starts with Pulse. From your first evolution your strongest family shapes your Strike: Grazers lash with a long tendril, Hunters fire fang darts, Swimmers surge through their targets, Guardians burst spines all around, Stalkers loose a silent needle. Every other family you grow adds its trait to whatever you hit: Grazer pulls and heals, Hunter poisons, Swimmer adds speed and range, Guardian knocks back and stuns, Stalker punishes unaware prey. Your Strike changes shape when another family overtakes your strongest.

    Grazer

    Feed on algae. Grow feeding cilia and recover more easily.

    Hunter

    Actively attack and hunt organisms. Merely drifting into small prey has much less influence. Grow jaws and venom.

    Swimmer

    Explore the pond and use sustained fast swimming. Grow flagella and become faster.

    Guardian

    Time successful blocks, use Shell and seek golden mineral clusters. Grow armour and Shell.

    Stalker

    Sneak near real predators and land concealed ambushes. Hiding beside harmless food does not count. Grow camouflage and decoys.

    Watch the five experience bars on the body screen to see which family grows next. Mixed habits create mixed creatures. This is a playful interpretation of evolution.

    Explore restorative blooms, sheltered sanctuaries, gene caches, predator nests and fast currents. Sanctuaries heal after you have shaken pursuit and avoided damage for a few seconds. Complete research to record your progress and earn score. Eating algae or protein restores an extra 1.5% of maximum health each second for six seconds. More food refreshes the duration without stacking the rate. This works everywhere, including during attacks and boss fights. Predators pursue and commit to lunges. They keep swimming while recovering stamina. Watch their movement, and choose when to turn, accelerate, conceal or fight. Smaller organisms seek vegetation; armoured scavengers gather around remains. Healthy predators remain dangerous even when they are smaller than you. Beyond four ranks, a family gains mastery.

    The elder waters.

    Three distinct elders awaken at 8, 64 and 512 cells. Each has attack warnings, multiple phases and openings to exploit. Every creature has a useful Strike, whatever path it follows.

    Claim one, two and three different elder genomes to grow beyond 16, 128 and 1024 cells. Biomass you gather while waiting is saved. Follow the elder compass, or use the field guide to plan your fight.

    A faint outer ring marks the encounter. Cross it to retreat and prepare; leaving lets the elder recover. A checkpoint is set on arrival, before combat. Retreating keeps that checkpoint. Defeating one permanently unlocks its genome and a unique mutation for this life.

    Automatic checkpoints are set when you begin a life, unlock a gene cache, nutrient bloom, predator nest or sanctuary, arrive at a sanctum, finish resting safely at a sanctuary, or defeat an elder. Location checkpoints retain your current health and stamina; unlocking does not grant a free heal. Simply discovering a distant location or riding a current does not save. At a sanctuary, recover your health and stamina, then settle for 3 seconds to set one checkpoint for that visit. Open navigation with N or the compass card to track a nearby sanctuary or one you have discovered. Attacking briefly suspends its shelter; let your attacks settle before resting. Ordinary travel, evolution and pausing do not change your checkpoint. Sanctums save once per arrival, before combat, rather than repeatedly during a fight. Use Continue on the title screen or Return to checkpoint after defeat. Loads pause until you are ready. Your checkpoint place and time appear beside your score. Starting a new life replaces that checkpoint. Save life from the pause menu to keep a separate file or move your creature to another device. File saves are specific to this build while the game is evolving.
