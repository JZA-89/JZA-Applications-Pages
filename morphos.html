    Wheel / + / −
    Adjust the view from 85% to 120%. Press 0 to reset. The camera keeps its automatic encounter framing; zoom never changes attack reach or movement speed.
    Mouse / arrows
    Swim towards the cursor, or move independently with the arrow keys while aiming with the mouse. Releasing an arrow stops movement without discarding your aim.
    Left click / Q
    Aim with the cursor and hold to repeat a forward Pulse. Targets behind you are outside its attack arc. Attack and Block have separate cooldowns. Aim towards an incoming attack and tap C to block.
    W · E · A · S · D
    Siphon, Venom, Slipstream, Shell and Decoy. Each organ unlocks with its evolution family and has its own cooldown. The keys always stay in the same places.
    C / Block
    Aim towards the attack and tap just before impact. Block lasts 0.16 seconds at first, costs 10 stamina and recharges in 2.5 seconds. A successful block refunds 8 stamina and teaches Guardian; pressing too early, facing away or holding the button does not protect you. Guardian adaptations return blocked attacks. Shell remains a separate, longer defensive ability.
    Hold right click
    Fast swimming drains stamina continuously. Release before empty to recharge immediately. Empty the bar and all stamina actions lock until it is full. Ordinary swimming always works. Your feeding crown folds during fast swimming: release to gather efficiently and engulf prey.
    Hold Space
    Sneak to reduce detection. Sneaking near real danger and successful ambushes shape a Stalker. Attacks and fast swimming reveal you.
    R
    Open field research, inspect the current study and follow mutation leads.
    N
    Choose discoveries, a sanctuary or the next elder. Navigation pauses the game.
    U
    Inspect your organism, improve core stats, compare mutations and follow research. The pond pauses while you choose.
    P / Esc
    Pause, save this life to a file, or load an earlier save. Growth and elder rewards pause the pond until you continue. Banked divisions are combined into one growth screen.

    Touchscreen
    Use the swim pad and ability buttons. Attacks aim towards nearby threats while you steer; tap Block to face an incoming shot. Hold Fast swim to accelerate. Tap Sneak to toggle concealment. The DNA icon opens your build.

    Evolution earns a stat point. Spend points on six core stats. Raise the rank limit as you divide; refund your choices at full health and stamina between elder encounters.

    Mutations are earned through specific actions or elder victories. Slots open at 4, 16 and 64 cells. You can inspect and preview every mutation in your Body screen, then equip discovered mutations between elder encounters. Previewing never changes your build.

    Grazer

    Feed on algae. Grow feeding cilia and recover more easily.

    Hunter

    Actively attack and hunt organisms. Merely drifting into small prey has much less influence. Grow jaws and venom.

    Swimmer

    Explore the pond and use sustained fast swimming. Grow flagella and become faster.

    Guardian

    Time successful blocks, use Shell and seek golden mineral clusters. Grow armour and Shell.

    Stalker

    Sneak near real predators and land concealed ambushes. Hiding beside harmless food does not count. Grow camouflage and decoys.

    Your next adaptation follows the strongest evidence from your behaviour. Watch the five live influence bars to steer your growth. Stored biomass retains the habits that earned it. Mixed habits create mixed creatures. This is a playful interpretation of evolution.

    Evolution pauses the game until you continue. Explore restorative blooms, sheltered sanctuaries, gene caches, predator nests and fast currents. Sanctuaries heal after you have shaken pursuit and avoided damage for a few seconds. Complete research to record your progress and earn score. Eating algae or protein restores an extra 1.5% of maximum health each second for six seconds. More food refreshes the duration without stacking the rate. This works everywhere, including during attacks and boss fights. Predators pursue and commit to lunges. They keep swimming while recovering stamina. Watch their movement, and choose when to turn, accelerate, conceal or fight. Smaller organisms seek vegetation; armoured scavengers gather around remains. Healthy predators remain dangerous even when they are smaller than you. Beyond four ranks, a family gains mastery.

    The elder waters.

    Three distinct elders awaken at 8, 64 and 512 cells. Each has attack warnings, multiple phases and openings to exploit. Every creature has a useful basic pulse, including grazers and swimmers.

    Claim one, two and three different elder genomes to grow beyond 16, 128 and 1024 cells. Biomass you gather while waiting is saved. Follow the elder compass, or use the field guide to plan your fight.

    A faint outer ring marks the encounter. Cross it to retreat and prepare; leaving lets the elder recover. A checkpoint is set on arrival, before combat. Retreating keeps that checkpoint. Defeating one permanently unlocks its genome and a unique mutation for this life.

    Automatic checkpoints are set when you begin a life, unlock a gene cache, nutrient bloom, predator nest or sanctuary, arrive at a sanctum, finish resting safely at a sanctuary, or defeat an elder. Location checkpoints retain your current health and stamina; unlocking does not grant a free heal. Simply discovering a distant location or riding a current does not save. At a sanctuary, recover your health and stamina, then settle for 3 seconds to set one checkpoint for that visit. Open navigation with N or the compass card to track a nearby sanctuary or one you have discovered. Attacking briefly suspends its shelter; let your attacks settle before resting. Ordinary travel, evolution and pausing do not change your checkpoint. Sanctums save once per arrival, before combat, rather than repeatedly during a fight. Use Continue on the title screen or Return to checkpoint after defeat. Loads pause until you are ready. Your checkpoint place and time appear beside your score. Starting a new life replaces that checkpoint. Save life from the pause menu to keep a separate file or move your creature to another device. File saves are specific to this build while the game is evolving.
